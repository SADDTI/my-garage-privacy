Delete Your Account & Data — My Garage

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your My Garage account and all data associated with it.

App / Developer

App name: My Garage
Developer: SADINCPASS

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to sad@mypassinc.com from the same email address you used to sign in with Google.
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete My Garage Account".
  3. We will verify your identity and process the request within a reasonable time.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account identity (name, email, profile picture obtained via Google Sign-In)
  • All vehicle expense records (fuel, service, repairs, insurance, tax entries) associated with your account

Vehicle information (make, model, year, insurance/inspection dates) stored locally on your own device is removed automatically when you uninstall the app, or you can clear it yourself by removing your vehicles in the app before requesting account deletion.

Retention

No data is retained after a deletion request is processed, except where retention is required by law.
