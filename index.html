Privacy Policy — My Garage

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the My Garage mobile application ("the App") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. My Garage helps you track your vehicles' expenses, insurance, and inspection dates.

The App is operated by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us"), who is the data controller for the personal data described below.

1. Information We Collect

Account information (via Google Sign-In): When you sign in, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account, used solely to identify your account within the App.

Content you provide: Vehicle details (name, brand, model, year, VIN, license plate, insurance and inspection dates) and expense records (category, amount, date, mileage, notes) that you enter into the App.

Automatically collected data: We use Google Analytics for Firebase, which may collect basic usage data (app interactions, device type, crash reports) and an app-instance or device identifier, to help us understand how the App is used and to fix problems. We do not use this data to identify you by name or email.

Payment information: Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details; we only receive information about whether your subscription is active.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and sync your vehicle and expense data across your devices
  • To send you local notifications reminding you of upcoming insurance or inspection deadlines
  • To maintain and improve the App's reliability and features
  • To manage free and premium subscription tiers

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your account content is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Access is restricted by security rules so that only you can read or modify your own vehicles and records — no other user of the App can see your data. Google may process data on servers located outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area, under its own safeguards and terms.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your information is only shared with Google (Firebase and Google Play), which act as our infrastructure and payment providers for authentication, data storage, analytics, and subscription processing, under their own privacy and security terms.

5. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the App schedules local reminders about your vehicles' insurance and inspection dates. This permission is optional; declining it only affects reminders and does not limit other App features.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us at the address below. We will delete your data within 30 days of a verified request. Some anonymized analytics data may be retained by Google under its own retention policies.

7. Your Rights (GDPR and Similar Laws)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region with similar data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • request deletion of your data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

We process your data to provide the App and its features (performance of a contract), to keep it secure and working properly (legitimate interests), and, where required, based on your consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children's Privacy

My Garage is not directed to children under 16 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
