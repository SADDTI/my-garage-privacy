Privacy Policy — My Garage

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the My Garage mobile application ("the App") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. My Garage helps you track your vehicles' expenses, insurance, and inspection dates.

1. Information We Collect

Account information (via Google Sign-In): When you sign in, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account, used solely to identify your account within the App.

Content you provide: Vehicle details (name, brand, model, year, VIN, license plate, insurance and inspection dates) and expense records (category, amount, date, mileage, notes) that you enter into the App.

Automatically collected data: We use Google Analytics for Firebase, which may collect basic usage data (app interactions, device type, crash reports) to help us understand how the App is used and to fix problems. This data is not linked to your name or email by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and sync your vehicle and expense data across your devices
  • To send you local notifications reminding you of upcoming insurance or inspection deadlines
  • To maintain and improve the App's reliability and features
  • To manage free and premium subscription tiers

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your account content is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Access is restricted by security rules so that only you can read or modify your own vehicles and records — no other user of the App can see your data.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your information is only shared with Google/Firebase, which acts as our infrastructure provider for authentication, data storage, and analytics, under their own privacy and security terms.

5. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the App schedules local reminders about your vehicles' insurance and inspection dates. This permission is optional; declining it only affects reminders and does not limit other App features.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, please contact us at the email address below. We will process deletion requests within a reasonable time.

7. Children's Privacy

My Garage is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
